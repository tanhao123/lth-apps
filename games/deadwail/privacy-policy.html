Deadwail – Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Developer: Lam Tan Hao · Windows and desktop web browsers

This policy covers Deadwail, a first-person horror game for adults by the developer Lam Tan Hao, on the Microsoft Store for Windows and in a desktop web browser on the LTH Games web store. You play alone, and you use your voice, through the microphone, as one of the controls. The game needs no account and does not ask for your real name, age or location. This page explains every piece of information the game uses and where it goes.

Stored on your device

The game saves on your own device only: your settings (screen size, volume, mouse speed, picture quality, reduced flashing), whether you have done the voice check and the tutorial, the microphone you picked, and what the voice check measured for that microphone (how loud your quiet room is and how low and high your voice goes, as plain numbers, never sound). On Windows the save is in the registry of your user account; on the web it is in your browser's storage. It is removed when you uninstall the game, or, on the web, when you clear this site's data in your browser. It is never sent anywhere.

The microphone

Deadwail is played with your voice: you moan, wail and shriek like the dead to pass among them. The microphone is part of the game, and you can play without it.

  • The microphone is opened only after you click to start it (NEW NIGHT, TUTORIAL or the voice check). Windows or your browser asks for permission first.
  • The sound is analysed on your device, a moment at a time, for its pitch, its length and how it moves, and thrown away. It is never recorded, stored or sent to the developer or to anyone else. The game has no chat and no voice to other players.
  • To play without it, choose NO MICROPHONE (the quiet mode) in the voice check, or press M in the game: then keys and the mouse do what the voice would do. You can also take the microphone permission away in Windows' or your browser's settings.

We suggest headphones, because the game's own sound can leak into the microphone.

What goes over the network

Windows. The Windows version has no online features. It needs no internet connection, makes no internet checks, has no feedback form and no account, and sends nothing about you or your play to the developer or to anyone else.

Web. The web version is loaded by your browser from the LTH Games web store. The game itself sends nothing about you or your play. The store sees the usual details of any web request (your IP address and browser), and the browser remembers which version of the game you played last, to keep you on the newest one.

Advertising

Windows: the Windows version shows no ads and uses no ad network.

Web: the game's own code shows no ads. The LTH Games web store's pages and the page the game plays on may show ads from Google (AdSense, and Google's ad breaks for browser games, for example before the game starts), and store small files (cookies and similar identifiers) in your browser. To serve, measure and protect them, Google may collect device identifiers, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), information about your device and how you interact with the ads. Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Google asks for your consent first. What these do is described in the LTH Games privacy notice, which also links to Google's explanation. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. The microphone is never used for ads.

Game engine diagnostics

The game is built with Unity. Unity may send anonymous technical details of your device, such as its model, operating system and graphics chip, to Unity Technologies to improve the engine. They do not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for players. Apart from the ads described above, the game uses no other analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

Deadwail has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to and close, and the developer holds no data about you. Your save is on your device: uninstall the game, or on the web clear this site's data in your browser. Ad data is held by Google: use Google's My Ad Center. To ask for anything else, email haolamtan@gmail.com with the subject “Deadwail: delete my data”.

Children

Deadwail is a horror game made for adults, with violence and blood, and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
