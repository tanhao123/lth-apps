Split Shift – Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Developer: Lam Tan Hao · Windows, Android, iOS and the web

This policy covers Split Shift, an online co-op game by the developer Lam Tan Hao in which a crew of friends works a night shift catching blobs, on the Microsoft Store, Google Play and the App Store, and in a web browser on the LTH Games web store. The game needs no account and does not ask for your real name, age or location. This page explains every piece of information the game uses and where it goes.

Stored on your device

The game saves on your own device only: the best run of nights you have reached, the room you were last in (so you can rejoin it), which hints you have already seen, whether you chose to play with the mouse instead of the microphone, the microphone you picked, and what the lung test measured for that microphone (how loud the quiet room, your puffs and your sips were, as plain numbers, never sound). It also keeps a random device token, described below. It is removed when you uninstall the game, or, on the web version, when you clear this site's data in your browser.

Online rooms

Split Shift is played in online rooms on the developer's server (on Microsoft Azure). A room handles only what is listed here.

  • A crew name, which the server makes up for you (for example “Dave from Night Ops”). You never type a name, and the other players in your room see only that one.
  • A random device token, made by the game and saved on your device, so that if you drop out you can rejoin the same room. It is not linked to you.
  • What you do in the room: moving, aiming, the tools you use, ready and ping. The server runs the shift and sends the other players what they need to see.
  • The game's version and the kind of platform (“WebGL”, “Windows”, “Android” or “iOS”), so that only matching versions meet in a room.
  • Your IP address, which the server uses to limit abuse (how many rooms and joins one address can make), in memory. The server's host keeps standard access logs, which include IP addresses, for 3 days and then deletes them.

Rooms live only in the server's memory. When a room closes, nothing about its players is kept. There is no account, no chat, and no voice is recorded or sent. Room codes and invite links let anyone who has them try to join while the room is open, so share them only with the players you want.

The microphone

Split Shift can be played with your breath: SIP to vacuum and PUFF to zap. This is optional. The mouse and keyboard, or the touch controls on a phone, always work, and the whole game can be played with them alone.

  • The microphone is used only if you allow it, and only in a room whose controls need it. Your device or browser asks for permission first (Windows, Android and iOS show their own prompt). A room whose controls are set to the mouse never opens it.
  • The sound is analysed on your device, a moment at a time, and thrown away. It is never recorded, stored or sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • The game's server receives only the same actions a click or a button would send (vacuum on or off, zap), and whether you are playing with the microphone or without it.
  • To switch it off, choose “Use mouse instead” in the game's settings, or take the microphone permission away in your device's or browser's settings.

We suggest headphones, because the game's own sound can leak into the microphone.

Checking for a new version

When it opens, the store versions of the game ask their store (the Microsoft Store, Google Play or the App Store) whether a newer version is available. The question only identifies the game. If there is a newer version, the game asks you to update before playing on.

Advertising

This version of the game shows no ads and uses no ad network. If a later version adds ads, this policy will say so first.

Web version

The web version runs in your browser from the LTH Games web store, and what it saves is kept in your browser's storage on your device. To keep you on the newest version, the browser also remembers which version of the game you played last. The web store's own pages may show ads (Google AdSense) and store small files in your browser: see the LTH Games privacy notice.

Game engine diagnostics

The game is built with Unity. Unity may send anonymous technical details of your device, such as its model, operating system and graphics chip, to Unity Technologies to improve the engine. They do not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for players. The game uses no other analytics or tracking tools.

Children

The game is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
